SphereBound Support

Help for the SphereBound iPhone game

Need help? Email Nabavijoe@gmail.com. Tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you’re using, and we’ll get back to you.

Restoring purchases

If you bought Remove Ads and ads came back — for example after reinstalling the game or moving to a new iPhone:

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  2. Open SphereBound → Settings → Restore Purchases.

Core Fragment packs are consumable and are used up in the game, so they can’t be restored. If a purchase was charged but you didn’t receive it, reopen the game while connected to the internet — it collects any purchases that didn’t finish. If it still hasn’t arrived, email us.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

SphereBound saves your progress on your iPhone only — there are no accounts or cloud saves. Deleting the game deletes your progress. You can start over at any time from Settings → Start over (this can’t be undone; Remove Ads is kept).

Ads and privacy choices

SphereBound shows occasional optional ads to support development. You can change your ad consent and tracking choices in Settings → Privacy Preferences, or turn ads off for good with the one-time Remove Ads purchase. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Something isn’t working

  • Close the game completely and reopen it.
  • Make sure your iPhone is on the latest iOS update and the game is up to date in the App Store.
  • Still stuck? Email Nabavijoe@gmail.com with what you were doing when it happened.
