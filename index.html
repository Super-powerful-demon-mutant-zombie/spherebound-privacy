SphereBound Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · applies from the version of SphereBound that adds ads and purchases

The short version: SphereBound has no accounts and saves your game progress only on your own device. We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal information. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data as described below, and offers optional in-app purchases handled by Apple.

Who we are

SphereBound is a mobile game developed by Joseph (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the SphereBound app handles information.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate any servers for SphereBound, and no information about you or your play is sent to us. The app has no user accounts, sign-in, or registration, and it does not use analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SphereBound shows ads to support its development: an occasional full-screen ad between Clashes, and optional ads you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped) and diagnostics.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices.

  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regions where required): before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy Preferences.
  • Tracking (iOS): the app asks for permission under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: an optional one-time purchase turns off all ads, and the ad SDK is not started for that device.

Purchases

SphereBound offers optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads, and packs of the in-game currency Core Fragments). Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app receives transaction information from Apple (such as the product and transaction identifier) and stores it on your device. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, SphereBound stores game data locally on your device: your Corelings, cards, story progress, discoveries, in-game settings (such as sound volume and haptics), and a record of your purchases and ad rewards. This data:

  • is never transmitted to us;
  • can be erased at any time from within the game (Settings → Start over) — your purchase record is kept so purchases can’t be lost or duplicated;
  • is removed when you delete the app (Remove Ads can be restored with Settings → Restore Purchases).

If your device’s own backup service (such as iCloud device backup) includes app data, that backup is handled by your device provider under their privacy policy, not by us.

Permissions

The only permission SphereBound may ask for is App Tracking Transparency (above). It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Children’s privacy

SphereBound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences with parental guidance. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad, contact us and we will help you reach Google to address it.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade information, because we do not receive any. Information collected by Google for advertising is handled by Google as described above; purchase information is handled by Apple.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of SphereBound changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
