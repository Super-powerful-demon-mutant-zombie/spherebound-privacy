SphereBound Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

The short version: SphereBound does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts and does not connect to the internet. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

SphereBound is a mobile game developed by Joseph (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the SphereBound app handles information.

Information we collect

None. SphereBound does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it does not send any data off your device. Specifically, the app:

  • has no user accounts, sign-in, or registration;
  • makes no network requests and works fully offline;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind;
  • does not use third-party services that collect data;
  • does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other device permission.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, SphereBound stores game data locally on your device: your Corelings, cards, story progress, discoveries, and in-game settings (such as sound volume and haptics). This data:

  • stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else;
  • can be erased at any time from within the game (Settings → Start over);
  • is removed when you delete the app.

If your device’s own backup service (such as iCloud device backup) includes app data, that backup is handled by your device provider under their privacy policy, not by us.

Purchases

SphereBound does not offer real-money purchases. In-game currencies are earned through play and exist only on your device.

Children’s privacy

Because SphereBound collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of SphereBound changes how information is handled — for example, by adding online features — we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
